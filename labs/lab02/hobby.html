My Hobbies #

My interest lies in good books especially history, fantasy, sci-fi and movies of the same genres. I don't like the term gamer but I do enjoy a good game when time allows - mostly those from turn of the century and early 2000s - the modern games are just interactive movies... unless we dive into indy creation.

I enjoy hiking and exploring new places but traveling I cannot say that I find pleasant (airports, long bus drives... etc.).

The biggest portion of my life belongs to computers.

I read a lot of technical books (usually two or three at the same time...) and I found all things unixy the most appealing to me - the simplicity and elegance. Software which is snappy, fast and with a low footprint is the one I use the most and I suffer using anything written by OOPs people...

I wrote my first lines of code in the last year of elementary school when we were introduced to Turbo Pascal.

At that point I was hooked to computers and I selected a high school with the programming curriculum as a mandatory class.

The animation on the right is my school project of choice - Sudoku generator and solver written in Pascal.

Later we got introduced into programming in language C, shell scripting and Linux at Brno University and I became Linux user ever since.
